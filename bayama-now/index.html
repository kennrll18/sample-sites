Frequently asked questions

Quick answers. For anything else, request a quote or call our team.

How long does carpet cleaning take to dry?

Most carpets are walk-dry within 4–6 hours with proper airflow. We share drying tips before we leave.

Are your products safe around pets and children?

Yes. We use family- and pet-conscious solutions. Keep paws and little feet off wet areas until fully dry.

Do you move furniture?

We move light pieces when needed. Heavy items stay in place; we clean around them or discuss options upfront.

How often should air ducts be cleaned?

Every 3–5 years is typical for many homes, or sooner after remodeling, heavy dust, or musty odors.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Share your rooms, surfaces, and concerns — we'll provide a clear quote before scheduling service.
